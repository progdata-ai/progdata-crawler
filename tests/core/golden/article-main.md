# How we bake our sourdough

Every loaf takes three days. On the first day we feed the starter with rye flour and let it rest in a cool corner of the bakery.

## Day two: the dough

We mix wheat flour, water and the starter, then fold the dough four times over two hours. The dough rests overnight at eight degrees.

- 1000 g wheat flour
- 750 g water
- 200 g starter
- 22 g salt

## Day three: the oven

The loaves bake for forty minutes in a stone oven. Read more in the [frequently asked questions](http://test.site/docs/faq.html).
